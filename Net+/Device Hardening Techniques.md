# Overview
Understanding the basics of network protection, specializing in the protection of the devices (the hardware itself) within the network.

# Purpose
Reduction of attack surface through the elimination of vulnerabilities and the implementation of information security practices
# Core Ideas
- **Constant variance of login credential**
- **Disabling of unnecessary services**
- **Updating firmware and software**
- **Implementation of strong encryption**
- **Segmentation of networks**
- **Enforcing Access Control Lists (ACLs)**
