# Overview
Understanding the different kinds of attacks a network can suffer through the focus on attacks made on the [[ARP]]

# Core Ideas
- The attacker corrupts the ARP cache of devices within the network, redirecting them to their device
- The result is altering the ARP Table of multiple devices, sending the data to one of their devices.

# Solution
- Detection capabilities on switches
- Deep ARP inspection
- **Legacy attack, mostly covered by newer switches**
