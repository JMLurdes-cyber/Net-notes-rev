# Overview
Short explanation of the IDF and how they are used as middle point between [[Main Distribution Frame (MDF)|MDFs]] and the end devices

# Core Ideas
- Usually set up with:
	- A [[Multimode Fiber]] coming from the MDF to give the connectivity.
	- A [[Ethernet|CAT6]] to connect to Desktops and Printers
	- Network [[Switches]]
- The size may vary. A "Full Sized rack" will have 42U, U referring to the devices per frame found.
