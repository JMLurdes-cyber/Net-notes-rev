- Also known as Gateways
- Can operate on the [[DATA LINK LAYER|data link layer]] and the [[Network Layer|network layer]]
- Creates a route between two [[Subnets|subnets]].
- Uses [[routing tables]] to decide where a packet should go.
- Forwards packets based on [[IP Addresses]].
- Requires a [[Default Gateway]] to connect with external networks. The IP of the Default Gateway must be on the same subnet as the Gateway.
- There are two main configurations for routing [[Static Routing]] and [[Dynamic Routing]]
