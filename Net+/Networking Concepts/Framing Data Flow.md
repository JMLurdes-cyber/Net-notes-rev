# Receiving vs Forwarding
- The destination and source [[MAC Addresses]] get reversed
- The destination and source [[INTRODUCTION TO PORTS|port]] and [[Common Ports|protocol]] get reversed too (in the payload)
- The [[Cyclical Redundancy Check|CRC]] get recalculated
- The [[Switches|switch]] also reverses the flow, but the [[MAC Addressing Table]] stays unchanged.
