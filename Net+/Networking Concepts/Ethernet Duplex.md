We call duplex to the kind of communication between a [[Switches|switch]] and the end workstation.

## Half Duplex
- Data can be sent and received, **but not simultaneously**
- Legacy system, is mostly used on older/ not-up-to-date devices

## Full Duplex
- Data can be sent and received **simultaneously**

Some of the more recent [[ETHERNET STANDARDS|standards]] of Ethernet will **require** a kind of duplex or another.
