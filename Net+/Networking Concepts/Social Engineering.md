# Overview
Set of techniques and methods meant to manipulate human behaviors and patterns into divulging or weakening security

# Core Ideas
- **Dumpster Diving**: Gathers discarded information from dumpsters or containers
- **Shoulder Surfing**: Observes passwords and pins entered
- **Tailgating**: Following an authorized person into a secure are without being authorized
- **Phishing**: Impersonation of  a person (either private or work-related) to reveal personal or financial information
