# Overview
Understanding the basics of [[networking documentation]] through an explanation of the operative life of the devices used in the network.

# Core Ideas
- **[[Patches]]**: software bug fixes, security vulnerability fixes, feature enhancements
- **Operative Systems (OS)**: Patches and feature updates
- **Firmware**: [[Internet of Things|IOT]] devices
- Decommissioning: Safe disposal, donation, recycling. Separated into two fields:
	- **End-of-life (EOL)**: The date when a product is no longer sold by the manufacturer. The product will still be patched occasionally, help is also offered. 
	- **End-of-support (EOS)**: The date when the vendor stops patching and offering help with a product. Atp, the product needs to be replaced or upgraded for safe use.
