# Overview
Management and documentation of changes made into the physical networking environment

# Core Ideas
 - We use [[ITIL]] (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) to perform IT service management (ITSM). It follows a set of clarifications:
	 - **Change Request**: Formal request that explains the scope of the change, reasoning and impact on the overall environment.
	 - **Change Control Board**: Responsible party who manage change requests
	 - **Change Implementation**: A method of rollback needs to be fully set-up before proceeding
	 - **Post-implementation review**: Understanding and documenting the result.
