# Concept
- A thick plastic tube that connects two devices in a network.

# Key Info
- Main method of wired connections.
- More reliable/faster than wireless connections.
- Also safer than [[WIRELESS NETWORKS|wi-fi]], since it IS directly connected to the device.
- Depending on the [[Types of Connections|media]] used, the user may adapt and use different lengths, speeds, etc.

# [[Types of Connections]]
- The three main kinds of Ethernet connections are:
	- [[COPPER CABLINGS|Copper]]
	- [[FIBER OPTICS| Fiber]]
	- [[WIRELESS NETWORKS|Wirelessly]]
