- Separated on [[DATA LINK LAYER|2nd layer]] and [[Network Layer|3rd layer]] switches.
- Both forward [[BASICS OF ENCAPSULATION|frames]] within a [[LAN]] by learning and using the [[MAC Addresses]] of the connected devices and save them into a [[MAC Addressing Table]]
- Only 3rd layer switches can also route using [[IP Addresses|IP addresses]], or between [[VLANs]]
- Depending on the settings/kind of cable connected, the connection can be [[Ethernet Duplex|duplex]] or [[Ethernet Duplex|half-duplex]].

## [[MAC Addressing Table]]
- Table created to keep tabs of all devices connected to a switch.

# [[BROADCAST|Broadcasting]]
- During Broadcasts, all the values of the [[MAC Addresses|MAC Address]] get replaced by "FF", which signalizes the switch to forward it to every connected device.
