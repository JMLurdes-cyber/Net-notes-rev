###### [[Service Level Agreement (SLA) |SLA]]
- Agreement that specifies the amount of the expected service, and the compensation if this is failed to be met.
###### [[Recovery Point Objective (RPO)|RPO]]
- Maximum acceptable data loss in time that an organization can tolerate after a disaster.
###### [[Recovery Time Objective (RTO)]]
- Maximum acceptable downtime that a business can have to recover critical operations and services
