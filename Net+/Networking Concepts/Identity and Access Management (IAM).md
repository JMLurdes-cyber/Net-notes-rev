# Overview
Framework of policies and technologies that ensure that the right individuals have access to the right resources at the right times, for the right reasons.

# Core Ideas
Follows the **AAA Framework**:
- **[[Authentication]]**: Verifies the identity of the user before granting access.
- **[[Authorization]]**: Determines the permissions that the authenticated user has.
- **[[Accounting]]**: Tracking, reporting and alerting of any authentication and authorization requests and responses.
