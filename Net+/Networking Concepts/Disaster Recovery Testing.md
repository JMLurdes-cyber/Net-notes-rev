# Overview
Set of training exercises performed to drill the correct steps to follow during a disaster.

# Core Ideas
| Tabletop                                                                | Validation                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Simulates DR scenarios in a discussion format                           | Real world testing of recovery processes to ensure meeting the [[Recovery Time Objective (RTO)\|RTO]] and [[Recovery Point Objective (RPO)\|RPO]] |
| Engages the personal in a walk-through of their roles during a disaster | Confirms the effectiveness of backups and [[Failover]] mechanisms                                                                                 |
| Helps refine procedures without disrupting the opreration               | Provides insights as to what to improve on the plan                                                                                               |
