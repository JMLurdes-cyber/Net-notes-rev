- **Network architectures** describes the structure of a computer network. This architecture includes:
	- What [[Networking Devices|devices]] are connected
	- [[Network Topologies|How]] are they connected
	- How does the data travel (routing, rules, priorities)
	- How the network is protected
- It defines **both physical and logical operations**.

- The two architectures explained on this course are:
	- [[Three Tiered Architecture]], used on businesses that require high performance, scalability and redundancy.
	- [[Collapsed Core]], used on smaller networks, combines [[Distribution Layer|distribution]] and [[Core Layer|core]] to reduce complexity/cost.
