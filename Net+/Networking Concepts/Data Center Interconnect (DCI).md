# Overview
- Management systems that allows the control, implementation and modification of all the protocols involved in a [[Data Center Interconnect (DCI)|DCI]].

# Core Ideas
-  Service provided by vendors like [[Cisco]], Juniper or HP in their networking sytems for [[Data Centers]].
- Called **Overlay** when it is implemented with a [[Virtual Extensible Local Area Network (VXLAN)|VXLAN]] through a [[Generic Routing Encapsulation (GRE)]] tunnel.
- This is one of the basis of [[Software Defined Networking (SDN)]].
