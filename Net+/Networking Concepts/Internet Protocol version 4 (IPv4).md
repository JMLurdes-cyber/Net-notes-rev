## Concept
- An explanation of the foundational, 32-bit addressing system used to identify devices on a network and route most internet traffic.

## Key Ideas
- Operates on the [[Network Layer]].
- One [[IPv4 Addresses|address]] is composed of  **32 bits**, or **4 bytes**.
- Since each byte is composed by 8 bits, the calculation of the [[Binary Math|binary value]] will always go up to 255, never over it.

| :------: | :------: | :------: | :------: |
|  8 bits  |  8 bits  |  8 bits  |  8 bits  |
| 11000000 | 10101010 | 10101010 | 10101010 |
