# Core Ideas
- Most enterprises use a [[Main Distribution Frame (MDF)]] as the central point of connections.
- There are usually [[Intermediate Distribution Frames(IDF)]] connected on mid-sections of the building, connecting the MDF cabling to the switches/devices found in the building.
- Usually **racks** will be locked or found in some sort of safe space to difficult access from unauthorized actors.
- One of the issues coming with **server racks** is the cooling, which needs to be constant to ensure functionality.
