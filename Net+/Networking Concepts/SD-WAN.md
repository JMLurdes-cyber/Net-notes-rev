# Overview
- Software Defined Networking of a [[Wide Area Network (WAN)|WAN]].

# Core Ideas
- Can be used with a [[WAN-link]] through the use of a [[dark fiber connection]], or a shared alternative like [[Ethernet]] or [[MPLS]].
- Allows for the simplified use of a remote site.
- Can also make use of [[VPN|VPNs]] or [[Load Balancer|Load Balancers]] to the facility.
- It ignores the route that the traffic takes to the building; it focuses on the amount of traffic and the time taken.
