# Overview
Attack that attempts to overwhelm the [[MAC Addressing Table]] of a [[switches|switch]] to force it into [[BROADCAST|broadcast mode]].

# Impact
- Allows attacker to :
	- capture all network traffic (also known as [[packet sniffing]])
	- perform [[Man In the Middle (MITM)|MITM]] attacks

# Mitigation
- Port security limiting the amount of [[MAC Addresses]] per port.
