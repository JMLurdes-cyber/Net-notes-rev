# Overview
Review of the [[Networking Documentation|documentation]] of networking operations. Focused on the documentation of assets


# Core Ideas
Separated into  types:

## Hardware Inventory
- All physical devices connected to the network
- [[Internet of Things|IoT]] based

## Software Inventory
- Operating systems
- Apps
- Utilities
- [[Shadow IT]]

## Licensing management
- Software licenses

## Warranty Support
- List of devices being/not being covered for repair or replacement

## Ip Address Management (IPAM)
- Managing the allocation and administration of [[IP Addresses]].

## Service-Level Agreement (SLA)
- Contractually defines service levels and credits
