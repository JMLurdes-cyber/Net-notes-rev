- Electronic devices that *send and receive data*.
- The type may change with the intended function.
- Two different types won't be compatible with each other.
- They are used over different media types, like [[FIBER OPTICS|fiber]] and [[COPPER CABLINGS|copper]]
- Some common transceivers are: 
	- [[SFP and SFP+]] 
	- [[QSFP and QSFP+]]
