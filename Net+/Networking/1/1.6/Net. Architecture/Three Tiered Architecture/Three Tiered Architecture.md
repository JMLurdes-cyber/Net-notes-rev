- Three Tiered Architecture **separates the architecture into 3 smaller layers**.
- Each layer is defined their **functionality**, not it's devices.
- Used to simplify design, separate responsibilities and improve scalability and reliability.


The three layers are:

# [[Access Layer]]
- Connects end devices to the network.
# [[Distribution Layer]]
- Manages the routing and flow of data

# [[Core Layer]]
- Moves traffic as fast and reliably as possible
