## Concept
- An special, reserved IP address that allows a computer to communicate with itself.

## Key Ideas
- Useful for testing, software development and troubleshooting
- Usually is found in the range 127.0.0.0 - 127.255.255.255, even though **127.0.0.1 is the standard**.
- Data sent to a loopback address doesn't travel through the internet.
- Always active.
