## Concept
- IP address refers to an unique number that identifies a device within a network.

## Key ideas
- Every device needs an unique address formed by a set of four [[Binary Math|bytes]].
- Each byte within the address is composed from numbers ranging between 0-255
- Since the bits are [[Binary Math|binary values]] (either 0 or 1), their theoretical maximum will always be an exponent of 2 (since it will always be one of **two** values).
- Two devices **on the same LAN** cannot share an IP.
- An example would be 255.125.122.12
