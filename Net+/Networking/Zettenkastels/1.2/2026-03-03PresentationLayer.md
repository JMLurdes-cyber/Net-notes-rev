## Concept
- The layer that takes care of the exchange of information between the human and the lower layers.

## Key Ideas
- Handles the translation between human-readable formats like ASCII and machine ones like binary.
- Handles the encryption of data during sending and the decryption when the data is being received.
- Handles the compression of data and the decompression when the data is being received.
