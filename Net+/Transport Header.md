# Overview
- An explanation of how the [[TRANSPORT LAYER|Layer 4]] makes its [[BASICS OF ENCAPSULATION|encapsulation]] through the use of [[TCP]] and [[UDP]]. 

# Core Ideas
- We call [[Transport Layer Segments|Segments]] to the packets encapsulated at this layer.
- The header will include:
	- Source Port
	- Destination Port
	- Type of transfer:
		- Will it be fast with [[UDP]]
		- Will it be secure with [[TCP]]
